Frequently Asked Questions

What services does AURA UX provide?

AURA UX specializes in two core domains: 1) Best for Brands (Corporate UX, bespoke websites, full-featured e-commerce stores, SaaS UI design, and 3D WebGL experiences) and 2) Digital Surprises (custom interactive birthday websites, candle blowout greeting portals, 3D anniversary proposals, memory scrapbook portals, and luxury digital invitations).

How do AURA UX Digital Surprises work?

Digital Surprises are bespoke, private interactive web experiences built for birthdays, anniversaries, and milestone celebrations. They include interactive features like 3D gift unboxings, candle blowout microphone triggers, personalized photo memory walls, love note storybooks, and audio voice note messages that can be easily shared with loved ones via a unique link.

Can AURA UX develop custom e-commerce and brand web platforms?

Yes. AURA UX builds high-performance e-commerce platforms and brand portals tailored to your exact aesthetic and transactional requirements, complete with responsive cart workflows, checkout engines, booking reservation systems, and fast performance.
